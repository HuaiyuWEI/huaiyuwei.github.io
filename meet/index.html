Tri-Zone Meeting Planner

Find a meeting slot across the US west coast, Israel, and Canberra. Each cell shows three comfort stripes — US | Israel | Canberra — for that hour. Mark when you're free, send your availability code back to the organizer, and the grid overlays everyone.

How to use this (collaborators, start here)
  1. Check the window: if you opened the organizer's link, the start date and length are already set from the link; otherwise enter the dates they named.
  2. Pick your view: "View hours in" defaults to your device's clock — the grid rows are your local hours.
  3. Mark availability: in Mark availability mode, click or drag across the hours you could meet. Your marks are saved in this browser.
  4. Share it: type your name. If live sync is on (noted in the Responses section below the grid), your marks reach everyone automatically. Otherwise click Copy my availability and email/Slack the code to the organizer, who pastes it into the Responses box.
  5. Read the grid: numbers show how many people are free at each hour and ★ marks the top slots.
